# CampusGreen Connect: Goal

## Vision

To build a safe, student-only campus marketplace where Filipino college students can buy, swap, and give away textbooks and school materials, making education more affordable and campus life more sustainable.

## Primary Goal

Create a trusted, verified, student-to-student platform for exchanging school items (textbooks, uniforms, supplies, dorm essentials) that reduces cost for students and reduces waste for the campus community.

## Objectives

1. **Safety and Trust**
   - Restrict access to verified students through institutional (.edu.ph) emails or Student ID photo verification (e.g., ADNU, UNC).
   - Build user confidence through Trusted Campus Badges and reputation scores based on verification status and transaction history.
   - Guide safe in-person exchanges with a Safe Meetup Zone Locator showing on-campus locations such as security posts and student centers.

2. **Affordability and Easy Discovery**
   - Help students quickly find the right materials by filtering items by course (e.g., BS Tourism Management, Nursing) and year level.
   - Offer multiple ways to get items: buy, barter, or receive for free.

3. **Sustainability**
   - Tag items by condition (Brand New, Slightly Used, Well-Loved) to set clear expectations.
   - Show a Green Impact Counter that tracks waste and carbon emissions saved through reuse.

4. **Community and Generosity**
   - Enable item-for-item barter trades through **The Swap Zone**, with no cash needed.
   - Support a **Pay-It-Forward / Freebie Corner** where graduating students and dormers donate items to underclassmen.

5. **Convenient and Secure Payments**
   - Support local digital wallets (GCash and Maya) alongside cash on physical meetups.
   - Provide optional Buyer Protection through escrow, holding funds until the buyer confirms receipt and the item's condition.
   - Centralize escrow funds in the Student Profile **Escrow Wallet & Cash-Out Hub**, allowing instant withdrawals to GCash and Maya with zero transaction fees for verified students.

6. **Seller Success**
   - Provide a dedicated Seller Hub within the Student Profile featuring growth tools to accelerate campus trades.
   - Empower sellers with **Listing Bumps** to boost active items back to the top of the discovery feed.
   - Enable **Urgent Selling Badges** to maximize visibility for students needing fast turnarounds before exams, clinical rotations, or dorm move-outs.
   - Surface real-time listing analytics (views, inquiries, basket saves) to help students manage price and demand.

## Target Users

- **Buyers:** Students looking for affordable textbooks and materials for their course and year level.
- **Sellers:** Students who want to sell, swap, or pass on items they no longer need.
- **Donors:** Graduating students and dormers giving items to underclassmen.

## Success Indicators

- Number of verified student users per partner campus.
- Number of completed transactions (sales, swaps, and freebies).
- Share of transactions using Safe Meetup Zones and escrow protection.
- Total waste and carbon emissions saved, as shown by the Green Impact Counter.
- Repeat usage and positive reputation scores among users.

## Guiding Principles

- **Students first:** every feature should lower cost or effort for students.
- **Safety by default:** verification and safe meetups come before growth.
- **Local fit:** built around Philippine campuses, courses, and payment habits.
- **Sustainable by design:** reuse is rewarded and made visible.
